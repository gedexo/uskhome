Refund Policy

Updated: 30 October 2026

1. Before payment

This is a digital subscription service. Review the price, duration and included features before paying. Contact us on WhatsApp before purchase if you have questions.

2. Situations we review

Report duplicate payments, debits without service delivery, failure to receive promised access or significant unresolved service issues. We review payment status and service delivery to determine an appropriate resolution or applicable refund.

3. How to request a review

Send your payment ID, date, amount, registered phone number and a description of the problem through WhatsApp below. You may share a receipt if needed; do not send confidential card details or an OTP.

4. Cancellation after access is provided

Cancellations due to a change of mind or non-use after account activation are subject to the condition in point 6 below. Payment or service issues described in point 2 may still be reported; applicable statutory rights remain available.

5. Approved refunds

If a refund is approved, we will communicate the amount and processing status. Where possible, it will be returned through the original payment method. Credit time depends on the bank or payment provider; this page does not promise a fixed processing time.

6. Refunds after account activation

Once your ID has been created and the branding design for your business has been completed and updated in your account, you can download more than 600 posters for your business. As posters and other digital content become available after account activation, refunds will not be provided once the account has been activated.
