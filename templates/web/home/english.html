THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PLATFORM

About us

A web login tool to help grow your revenue!

USK Agent Login is a B2B (business-to-business) web application created for online service centres by the startup Bruvsha LLP.

Bruvsha LLP has received DPIIT Startup Recognition and Kerala Startup Mission Unique ID certification.

The platform combines technology with easy-to-use features to help businesses deliver better services at a lower cost and access posters for their marketing.

Learn more about our journey

As digital threats evolve, our team recognised the need for strong authentication and a user-friendly login experience. USK Agent Login began with a mission to improve the online services sector.

We continue to update and improve the platform to serve users better. Our aim is to introduce new features that help your business move to the next level as needs change.

LET’S MAKE IT CLEAR

Have questions?

What to know before you get started.

What is USK LOGIN?

It is a B2B web application that helps online service centres increase revenue and simplify their work by providing marketing posters, service links, forms, tutorials and more.

Is USK LOGIN a government website?

No. It is a private business support web platform. Official websites for government services are separate.

How do I pay?

Use the offer button to access the payment link provided by USK Login. Review the current amount and terms before paying.

Is the ₹2,499 price still available?

The regular subscription charge is ₹3,499. During the special-offer period, the service is available for ₹2,499. Click the offer button to check whether the offer is currently available.

Do I need design skills?

No special design skills are needed. Video tutorials and a digital guide are available to help you get started.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. USK Agent Login is a web application designed for mobile phones and computers.
